- **Fix: your progress reaches the server again.** In 1.2.0 the Infinity hook (0.3.0+fh2online.97) misread whether the server keeps your profile in the cloud, so the game's online services (profile sync, scores, ghosts, drivatars) were answered on your PC and never reached the server. Your saves on this PC were not affected. 1.2.1 (hook 0.3.0+fh2online.98) fixes it; update and reinstall the hook when the launcher asks.

Everything else is as in [1.2.0](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/releases/tag/v1.2.0). Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v1.2.1#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
