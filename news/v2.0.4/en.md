- **Show me on the website.** The Account page has a new Website card: one switch per profile chooses whether your name, stats, garage and records appear on [play.infinity-horizon.com](https://play.infinity-horizon.com). It is on by default; turn it off to keep a profile off the site.

The hook is unchanged (0.3.0+fh2online.118). Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v2.0.4#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
