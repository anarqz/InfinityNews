- **Azimuth 1.0.4 and ExtraOptions 0.4.4.** Infinity now runs on Azimuth 1.0.4 with ExtraOptions 0.4.4. Azimuth 1.0.3 is no longer supported: download Azimuth 1.0.4 and point the launcher at it when it asks.
- New from ExtraOptions 0.4.4, in game: the Vignette Effect (HUD settings, on by default), an hour slider in photo mode, and headlight beams that follow the headlight toggle.

The hook is 0.3.0+fh2online.104: reinstall it when the launcher asks. Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v1.2.8#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
