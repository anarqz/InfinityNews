- **Everyone is an Infinity Racer.** Every feature is open to every player. Infinity Supporters, who helped build the project, carry a small Supporter badge. The launcher shows "CONNECTED - INFINITY RACER" (or "INFINITY SUPPORTER").
- **Every lobby, in one list.** The overlay's Lobbies tab opens on All: lobbies that match your mode and mods come first, closest first; the others follow, dimmed, with the reason next to them. Any player can open any kind of lobby (Everyone, Supporters, Friends, Private).
- **FREE ROAM ONLINE finds a lobby for you.** Without a lobby, picking FREE ROAM ONLINE joins the closest lobby that matches your mode and mods, or opens a new one for everyone. New lobbies are named "{your name}'s lobby".
- **Server status.** The server list, the launcher's sidebar and the in-game overlay show players online, Hardcore and Noob players (with registered profiles) and the number of open lobbies. The overlay's guide also shows your Discord picture when your account is linked.
- **Cloud saves follow the server.** When the server keeps a profile's saves in the cloud, the launcher says so (Noob: saved right away; Hardcore: each save is checked first), and the game's online services, drivatars included, reach the server for that profile.

Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v1.2.0#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
