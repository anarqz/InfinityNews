- **King: a second round crowns again.** A second King or Infected in the same lobby now starts with a crowned car (or patient zero) and keeps the event's rules and car restrictions.
- **King: the crown moves until the very end.** Hits in the last seconds of a match now take the crown, and matches longer than 4 minutes run their full length.
- **Infected alone no longer gets stuck.** An Infected event with only one player ends right after the start instead of leaving the leaderboard waiting.

The hook is unchanged (0.3.0+fh2online.118). Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v2.0.2#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
