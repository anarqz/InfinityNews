# Play offline without signing in

Launcher **1.0.10** opens on a new first screen with **Sign in** and **Play offline**.

- **Play offline** gives you the whole launcher without an account: profiles, Mods and offline play.
- Offline, the launcher and the game make **no requests to Infinity's servers**.
- Profiles you create offline stay on your PC. When you sign in later, you can upload them to your account (up to three).
- The button on the Play tab says **PLAY OFFLINE** or **PLAY ONLINE**, so you always know which one you're starting.

Signing in is still needed for online play.
