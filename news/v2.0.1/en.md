- **Proximity voice chat.** Hosts can now choose the lobby's voice mode in the overlay: Off, Open or Proximity. In Proximity you hear the players near you (up to 150 m), from the side they are on, louder when close and with a touch of Doppler as cars pass.
- **Team events.** Team races now have real red and blue teams, and during a team event racers hear only their own team ("Team chat").
- **Voice settings and players.** Overlay settings are organized in categories (Connection, Voice Chat). Each player in the lobby has a big mute button beside their card; their volume is in the player's details.
- **More reliable voice.** Changing your audio devices mid-game no longer risks freezing the game, any microphone format works (including Bluetooth headsets), and with no microphone you can still listen: the overlay never sends your speakers' sound.
- **Voice moderation.** Mutes are reported to help keep lobbies friendly, and a player blocked from voice chat is told so.
- **Connections.** The server now decides how each lobby connects (the "Always connect through the relay" option is gone); if the relay can't be reached, you are connected directly to the host when possible.
- **Fair play.** Hardcore lobbies check impossible speeds per car and impossible parts.
- **Mods.** Mods that don't change gameplay (Spotify Radio, Extra Options - Garage Revving) no longer need to match in a lobby and are allowed in every mode.
- **Levels.** Save and Discord levels now show your real driver level.
