Last message for the day.

If you got into the game and saw the little pink pop-up at the bottom of the screen, you're in.

To keep Infinity compatible with future Forza games, online matchmaking works a little differently.

## Hosting

1. Open the Infinity overlay (hold **VIEW** for about one second).
2. Create a new lobby there and choose **private** or **public**.
3. Back in the game, go to **SOCIAL → ONLINE FREE ROAM**.

## Joining

1. Open the Infinity overlay (hold **VIEW** for about one second).
2. The **Lobbies** menu lists lobbies close to you. If nothing shows up nearby, switch the filter to **GLOBAL**.
3. To join a private lobby, enter the code the host gave you.
4. After joining, go to **SOCIAL → ONLINE FREE ROAM** in the game and join the lobby's free roam.
