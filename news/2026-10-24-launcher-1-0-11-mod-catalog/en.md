Launcher **1.0.11** brings the official mod catalog: [release on GitHub](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/releases/tag/v1.0.11).

## The official catalog

Open **Mods** to see Extra Options, Nitro System and the Remix Car Pack. Every download is checked against the hash listed in the catalog before anything is installed, and mods that run code bring the Infinity mod runtime with them automatically.

## Remix Car Pack with your own files

The Remix Car Pack is listed in the catalog, but Infinity never distributes its files. Choose **Set up…**, point the launcher at your copy of the pack (its folder, any folder above it, or its ZIP) and the launcher checks every file and installs it. You can move or delete your copy afterwards.

## See what changes before you install

Before installing or updating any mod, the launcher shows which game folder it installs into and which files it replaces. Replaced files are backed up and restored when you disable or uninstall the mod.

## Extra Options is optional

You can play without Extra Options. Mods that need it, like Nitro, install it with them.

## Use a different catalog

Configure → Mod catalog lets you point the launcher at another catalog. The launcher always warns you that it's unofficial and unverified.
