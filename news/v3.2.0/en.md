- **See other players' Underglow and ANNA car parts.** In online free roam, other players' Underglow, plus the doors, hood, trunk, lights and turn signals they open or switch on with FH2 ANNA, now show on their car for everyone who has the same mod. Players without the mod see nothing different.
- **Day headlights are per car.** Turning your headlights on in daylight no longer lights up traffic cars of the same model.

Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v2.0.4#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
