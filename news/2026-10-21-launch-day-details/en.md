A quick additional update for today's launch.

## Start from your current save

Infinity lets you create a new profile from your current Azimuth save game, so you can bring your progress straight into your races with friends.

## Lobbies and events

You can join public and private lobbies, find lobbies near you (low latency) and take part in races and events with other players.

## Liveries and tunes in sync

Liveries and car tunings are synced between players in a lobby, so everyone sees everyone's car exactly as it should be.
