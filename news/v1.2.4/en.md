- **Marketplace, Social, Clubs and Rivals are on.** The pause menu's online tabs are now enabled by default and are yours to switch on the Mods page (ExtraOptions). If you had them off because Infinity used to keep them off, they are turned on once after this update.
- **No Treasure Map on Hardcore.** A Hardcore profile always plays with the Treasure Map off; your setting comes back when you play any other profile.

The hook is 0.3.0+fh2online.99: reinstall it when the launcher asks. Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v1.2.4#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
