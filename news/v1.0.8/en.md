- A lobby you host with nobody else in it now warns you after 4 minutes and closes after 5. Any player, invite or pending request keeps it open.
- Lobbies check for joining players more often only while someone is connecting, and check less often otherwise.
- Background checks of your lobby seat are less frequent when nothing is changing. Actions you take in the overlay still update right away.

Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v1.0.8#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
