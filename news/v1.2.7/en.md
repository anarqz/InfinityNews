- **Sharing liveries works.** Large designs no longer fail with "error communicating with the Forza Horizon 2 server": the game sent an extra copy of the paint that went over its own size limit, and Infinity now leaves it out. Previews over 190 KB are scaled down to fit, and a design whose preview the game cannot read is shared without one.
- **Your livery on Discord.** When you have shared a livery for the car you are driving, your Discord card shows it as its large image. Turn it off with "Show my livery" in Settings → Discord.
- **Lobbies open to Hardcore and Noob.** When you create a lobby, choose "Open to Hardcore and Noob" to let both modes in; such lobbies carry a Hardcore + Noob badge. The default stays your own mode only.
- Older designs saved before October 27 were never uploaded: open and save them again in the paint shop before sharing.

The hook is 0.3.0+fh2online.103: reinstall it when the launcher asks. Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v1.2.7#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
