- **Mods page, tidier:** one slim toolbar row, the search under it, and two views: **Catalog** (the mods Infinity installs and checks) and **Manual (N)** (the mods you copied into the mods folder yourself, as compact rows, with Open folder).
- **Your mod settings stay:** uninstalling a mod keeps its settings file, so installing it again brings your settings back. Remove from this PC deletes the kept mod files, never the settings.
- Every mod now loads through ExtraOptions; the old Infinity mod loader is gone (no mod in the catalog used it).
- The game files are updated the next time you press PLAY.

Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v2.0.4#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
