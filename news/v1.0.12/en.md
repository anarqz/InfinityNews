- **News in the launcher.** A News page and the latest stories on the PLAY screen, published from the official Infinity News feed. Open a story to read it in full, with images, formatting and emoji. A dot marks the news until you have opened every new story, and it remembers what you have read across restarts. The last copy stays readable offline.
- **Clearer mod states.** A mod that needs another one first now says which, and its button installs that mod (or enables it) through its own preview. A mod others depend on stays "Installed · On" and names them. A mod that needs a newer Infinity hook says so, instead of "Review required".
- **Fixes:**
  - controller and keyboard focus no longer draws a dashed box around the news;
  - Escape and the controller's Back button leave a story.
- The licences of the news feed's Markdig library and the Noto Emoji shapes are included in NOTICE.

Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v1.0.12#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
