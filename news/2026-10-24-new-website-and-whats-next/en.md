Infinity's new website is up and running: [infinity-horizon.com](https://infinity-horizon.com). I'll keep updating it through the day, so don't mind if anything looks off.

The next minor update arrives tomorrow with a few stability patches and some more relevant changes:

## Offline play mode

The Infinity launcher stops being an online-only mod and starts moving towards being your Forza hub for playing Forza games through Azimuth: managing mods, configuring games and playing online properly, without messing with files all over your computer or running random commands in a terminal. I'll keep working towards this goal alongside everything else.

## Mod platform

A proper mod runtime was written so modders can adapt their mods to Infinity's requirements, and the website will also host the growing catalog from now on.

## Player profile

The in-game option to show a gamercard now opens the overlay with details about that player. More features to come.

## Next week's major release: Infinity Worlds

I'm working towards letting players host their own worlds through matchmaking, entirely bypassing the need for Infinity's servers to share stats and records. That's what we're all waiting for.

In short, players in the same lobby share their profile stats, so they can see each other's ghosts, stats and records during that match. Leaving the lobby takes you back to your vanilla offline world.

As always, expect bugs.
