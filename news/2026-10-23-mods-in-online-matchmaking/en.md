Today we start testing mods in our online matchmaking, car mods included. That means players can use a set of custom mods in their lobbies and still play together.

At first we'll start with some of the most famous car remixes, still to be defined.

## The launcher manages your mods

To keep matchmaking fair and stable for everyone, the launcher is the central piece for managing your mods. Adding mods by throwing random files into your game folder will **never** be supported in Infinity. It might work, but it's absurdly unstable.

You'll only be able to use the mods you chose through the launcher.
