- **Idle seats go to players who wait.** The Infinity hook (0.3.0+fh2online.88) now tells the server whether you are driving, in a menu or away, on the game's own regular check. If you stay away while players wait for a seat, the overlay warns you before the seat goes to the next in line. Your game keeps running and your progress keeps saving; press Play again to rejoin the queue.
- **Fix:** signing in again shortly after choosing Play offline now always checks the service status (it says "checking" while it waits).
