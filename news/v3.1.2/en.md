- **Mods never stop you from joining.** Players with different mods can join each other again: in worlds, quick join, private lobbies, Free Roam and join requests. Your mods are only shown to others.
- **HARDCORE handles mods for you.** Pressing PLAY HARDCORE no longer refuses because of mods. The launcher turns off every mod of that game except the official car packs and mods that change nothing for other players (such as Spotify Radio and Garage Revving), and turns them back on at your next NOOB play. Those mods don't make a session "modded", so HARDCORE results are kept.
- **NOOB:** any mod works. With a mod that counts as modded, PLAY says the session's results aren't recorded.
- **Enable all / Disable all** for each game on the Mods page.
- **PLAY no longer gets stuck.** Questions asked while the game is starting (such as "This PC and the cloud have different progressions") now show on top and can be answered. After 20 seconds you can cancel the launch. Every stop says why.
- **Forza Motorsport 5:** careers from save files without a transaction log now copy, play and sync.
- **Retired mods** are turned off for online play instead of blocking it.
- Clearer messages when a mod's folder is missing or a car pack folder lacks a file.

Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v2.0.4#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
