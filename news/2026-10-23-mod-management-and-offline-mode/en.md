Adding mods to the game will be easy as hell from now on.

## Mods managed by Infinity

I've built a proper mod management system into the launcher, so your mods can be completely managed by Infinity. Lobbies also reduce the chance of joining a match with mismatching mods.

- Safely swapping mods
- Linked dependencies
- Clean removal when you don't want a mod anymore

All batteries included. No more downloading zips, hunting for folders, copying and pasting. Modders just need to add a small adapter to their mods and release them on Infinity's main repository. I'll share more about this over the weekend.

## Offline mode is coming

I'm also bringing an offline mode, so people who don't want to sign in can play without contacting Infinity's servers. Maybe one day Infinity is the main way of playing Forza games? Who knows?

## Why do you need an account to play online?

Someone asked why you need an account to play.

Playing online games can be dangerous. Infinity only lets you play in lobbies with a verified email account. That alone doesn't guarantee safety, but it's one more layer of security for you degenerates. Other mods let anyone join and find matches anonymously, an approach I personally don't enjoy.

A verified account (along with some future additions) is about keeping things under control. That's why you need one to play online with others.

Play safe! See y'all on the streets of Infinity!
