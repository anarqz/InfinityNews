- **Online Free Roam events are back.** Race and event markers show on the map again in online Free Roam. ExtraOptions 0.6.0 had hidden them; Infinity now keeps the game's own event switches online. Offline "Show All Events" works as before.
- **A launcher that never freezes.** A start screen appears at once, and the launcher opens on PLAY with your last game and careers ready. Slow work runs in the background: small jobs show in the bottom-right corner, bigger ones show what is happening on screen. After a session, PLAY waits while your cloud save uploads.
- **Clear feedback on PLAY.** "Joining queue…", then the queue, then "Joining game…" until the game opens.
- **Mods.** Mods are read in the background when the launcher starts. Each game has its own Mods page (Forza Horizon 2 / Forza Motorsport 5), shared with PLAY's choice. Install, Enable and Disable on a card act right away. Forza Motorsport 5 shows all its ExtraOptions options, and takes mods you copy in yourself (NOOB only). A blocked mod action always says why, and the "Interrupted mod operation" panel always has a button that clears it.
- **Up to 100 multiplayer mods** in a session (was 32).

The hook is updated (0.3.0+fh2online.126). Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v2.0.4#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
