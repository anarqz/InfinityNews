Later today, we'll begin a small closed beta to test the new cloud infrastructure with real players, then gradually open access.

**Getting FH2 online was our first milestone, and we reached it in less than two days.** People were already playing together last week. But Infinity has always been about more than connecting players: we want an authentic, fair experience, with as little manipulated progression as possible.

## Hardcore is the heart of Infinity

Cloud progression, shared stats, and racing alongside players following the same rules. Actually fighting for that last thousandth of a second against that damn Audi you still can't believe took the corner that fast.

## NOOB mode is staying too

Online sessions, livery sync, races, or just free roaming with a bunch of nerds and showing off your existing saves — it's all part of the plan.

## Modded cars? Absolutely

We're working toward a centrally managed game database so everyone in a session has access to the same supported content, keeping things consistent and fair. And when it comes to cars, we want to bring as many as we can.

## What about security?

Private matches and informed choices about public lobbies matter to us. No online system is risk-free, so during this early phase, I recommend playing with friends or people you trust from the Discord server.

Infinity will also include a crash-reporting system to help identify what went wrong and let you share diagnostics with us, including relevant information about the lobby you were in. These reports will help us investigate issues and take action against accounts deliberately harming the community. For now, **be cautious about joining unfamiliar public lobbies**. Private matches with trusted players are the safer choice.

## What about other multiplayer mods?

Other projects offer different approaches and features, and there's room for all of them. New tools, including AI, have helped talented people bring their ideas to life faster, which is great for the community. Ultimately, it comes down to **which community you want to call home.**

## Built beyond FH2

Infinity's core systems are being designed independently of any single Forza Horizon game, so we can adapt them to future Forza titles that Azimuth brings to PC — with the same account and a shared foundation for progression and online features.

## Want to support the project?

Donations will help cover ongoing cloud costs and keep Infinity sustainable and accessible. I'll share more about that in the coming days. Stay tuned.

Thanks for your patience while everything takes shape. Keep the suggestions and bug reports coming — they help us build a stable, fair, and welcoming place to race.
