- **Mods that depend on each other update together.** When a mod's update needs a newer version of another mod (or another mod needs this one's update), the launcher now offers both updates at once, in the right order, and checks every step before it changes anything. Before, each update was refused on its own and the button stayed off.
- **Clearer reasons when an update waits.** A mod card that cannot update yet names the other mod and what it needs.
- **PLAY checks mod dependencies.** If an enabled mod needs another mod that is missing, turned off or at a different version, PLAY now stops and tells you which mods and what to do, instead of starting the game with a mod that cannot work.
- **Folder settings for mods.** Mods can now ask for a folder, for example a music folder. Choose it in the launcher's own picker, clear it, or open it from the mod's settings.
- **Readable choices in mod settings.** Dropdowns show each choice's name instead of its internal value, and mods can add buttons that open their own folders.

Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v2.0.4#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
