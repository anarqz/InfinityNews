- **Infinity on your Discord profile.** While Infinity runs, your Discord status shows your profile (mode, club tag, name, level), credits, your current car and what you are doing: in a lobby (with the party size), in free roam, in the menus or in the launcher. Turn it off, or hide any part of it, in Settings → Discord.
- **Join a friend's lobby from Discord.** A "Join lobby" button appears on the card of a player in a public, Supporter or friends lobby with a free seat (never a private one). Links open Infinity, or paste one in "Join from link" (the way on Steam Deck). You still press Join, and the usual rules apply: a lobby of the other mode offers switching to your profile of that mode.
- If you use Bullet4fun's Discord RPC mod, the launcher tells you once that it now duplicates the built-in presence.

The hook is 0.3.0+fh2online.100: reinstall it when the launcher asks. Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v1.2.5#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
