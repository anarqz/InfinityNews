- Play offline without an account. The first screen offers SIGN IN, and "Play offline" under it. Signing in is only needed for online play.
- Offline, the launcher makes no request to Infinity's servers at all. Only GitHub is contacted, for the mod catalog, mod downloads and launcher updates. The service status is checked only after you click Sign in.
- Offline profiles live on this PC: up to 3, Noob only. Hardcore profiles are created while signed in. Each offline profile keeps its own save folder.
- When you sign in, "Update online profiles" offers to put this PC's profiles on your account, and you choose when there isn't room for all of them. Noob saves stay on this PC. Online Hardcore careers start from the server's seed; offline Hardcore progress stays offline.
- The launcher remembers that you played offline: the next start opens offline with your last profile selected, without signing in or contacting the servers.
- The in-game overlay works offline. It shows "Playing offline", and its Lobbies and Friends tabs say "Offline mode".
- Online-only areas (Account, bug reports, service status) show "Offline mode" instead of failing.
- Quit game works during offline play, from the launcher and from the overlay. After an offline game closes, nothing is synced.
- When a newer launcher is on GitHub, offline players see a note with a link to the official release page.
- Play explains why it waits: the game setup, a mod catalog that is loading or couldn't be loaded (with Try again), or a required mod.
- Every icon now ships with the launcher, so check marks, arrows and drop-downs draw correctly on Linux and Steam Deck.
- Fixes: a drop-down in mod options that drew incorrectly; small windows (the sidebar scrolls, long buttons wrap).
- The Infinity hook 0.3.0+fh2online.86 brings offline play; the launcher offers the update.

Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v1.0.10#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
