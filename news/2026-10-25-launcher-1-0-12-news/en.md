Launcher **1.0.12** is out: [release on GitHub](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/releases/tag/v1.0.12).

## News in the launcher

What you're reading now: Infinity's news shows up on the right of the Play tab and on the News page, in English and Portuguese. It's read straight from GitHub, so it also works offline — an unread dot tells you when something new arrives.

## Clearer mod states

Mods now say exactly what they're waiting for: "Needs Extra Options first", with an **Install Extra Options** button right on the card, or "Update the Infinity hook first" for mods that change the game database.

## Smaller fixes

Clearer error messages that name the catalog's address, the exit reason in the launcher log, and correct singular texts like "1 file".
