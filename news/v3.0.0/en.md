### Forza Motorsport 5 support (experimental)
- FM5 joins Infinity! For now it's private matches between friends only.
- Cloud saves for FM5 Noob profiles (experimental).
- Your Noob and Hardcore profiles are now shared across games: pick a game, then pick a profile.

### Official Lobbies and Worlds (experimental)
- Lobbies no longer depend on your connection. Every lobby is created and managed automatically on Infinity's servers, and everything is handled through the in-game menus.
- Room for up to 100 players: while the server has room, you always get a lobby.
- Proximity voice chat in Forza Horizon 2 official lobbies: you only hear players within 150 m, louder the closer they are.
- Private lobbies: the leader decides who can join (everyone, Hardcore only or Noob only).
- This is rolling out to everyone at once, so everyone joining is part of the experiment. Expect bugs!

### Friends, everywhere
- New FRIENDS sidebar in the overlay: see who's online, what they're doing (free roam, car meet, road trip, race) and their Discord avatar.
- "{friend} is online" notifications.
- New invite system: invite friends to your game or join theirs with one click. Ask to join a friend's private lobby and the leader can accept.
- Manage friends from the launcher or the overlay.

### Launcher updates
- Up to 80% smaller.
- New profile management and troubleshooter.
- New game picker based on the games you have installed.
- Start a Hardcore career from your other profile: your save is checked on the spot, and if it's clean you're good to go.

### New Explorer for Forza Horizon 3 and Forza Motorsport 5
- Maps, cars and events, now on [play.infinity-horizon.com](https://play.infinity-horizon.com/), with a new game selector at the top of the page.

### Known issues
- Showdown races are still a work in progress.
- Road Trips may still fail sometimes.
- On Linux (Proton), the game may freeze when leaving a lobby.
- Cloud saves for Forza Motorsport 5 are still experimental.
- FM5 invites can only be accepted from the Multiplayer screen. If you get one elsewhere, go into Multiplayer to accept it.
- Leaving and joining lobbies can leave you stuck between lobbies on an endless "Joining Session…". Restart the game if that happens.
- Stuck in a lobby? Press LB+RB to leave it.

Thanks for playing and for every bug report. Keep them coming!
