- **Infinity Horizon is out of beta:** no more BETA labels, badge or notice in the launcher.
- **ExtraOptions 0.6.1** (ExtraOptions.Infinity `0.6.1+dae2380`): addon cars no longer make the game stutter (mods' loose files are indexed once), Show All Events brings one-off races back after their championship, and the missing Storm Island weather presets are added. The game files are updated the next time you press PLAY.
- **Garage Revving and Weather Control**, which ExtraOptions 0.6.1 bundles, are installed from Infinity's Mods page as before (the launcher package does not include ExtraOptions' bundled mods).
- **DONATE** opens Infinity Horizon's Ko-fi page.
- The official mod catalog now comes from InfinityMods only.

Windows installations can use the launcher updater or extract the Windows x64 ZIP into a separate launcher folder. On Linux/Steam Deck, the launcher updater applies this release; if it cannot, rerun the [Linux/Steam Deck installer](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/tree/v2.0.4#install-on-linux-and-steam-deck).
