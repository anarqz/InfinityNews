Launcher **1.0.9** is out: [release notes on GitHub](https://github.com/anarqz/InfinityHorizon/releases/tag/v1.0.9). If the auto updater doesn't work for you, download it and use it from now on.

## Mods play online

Tested online with modded cars and Nitro. Players with mods that affect multiplayer meet in lobbies with exactly the same mods.

![Mods in the launcher](assets/mods.png)

## Modded lobbies

Lobbies created while mods are active show up under the **Modded** filter in the overlay. I'll keep working hard to bring more mod support and car packs to Infinity Horizon.

## New overlay shortcut

If holding VIEW didn't open the overlay for you, hold **LB + RB** for about one second.

> Update: since launcher 1.0.11 the Remix Car Pack is in the official mod catalog. You no longer unzip it by hand: open Mods, choose the pack's folder, and the launcher does the rest.
